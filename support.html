Support

We're happy to help. Email kitchery.support@gmail.com and we usually reply within two working days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Kitchery and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the app asks me to subscribe again

On the subscription screen tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data? Can I delete it?

Everything stays on your iPhone. To delete it, open You › Delete all my data, or delete the app.

Allergies

Kitchery leaves out recipes with the allergens you select, but always check product labels, because brands vary.
