Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Kitchery is a meal planning app. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it. The short version: your meal plans, grocery lists, prices, recipes and settings stay on your device. We do not run ads, we do not track you, and you don't need an account.

Information stored on your device

Your household settings, food preferences, allergies you choose to enter, meal plans, grocery lists, prices you type in, receipts totals, your own recipes and what the app learns about your taste are stored only on your device. We cannot see this information. Deleting the app, or using You › Delete all my data, removes it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device and your purchase information (such as the product, purchase date and expiry). It is not linked to your name, email or identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or health data.

Children

Kitchery is intended for adults planning meals for their households. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health information

Allergy and diet settings are only used on your device to filter recipes. Always check ingredient labels. Kitchery is not medical or nutritional advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
