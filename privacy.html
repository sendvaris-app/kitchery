Kitchery

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Kitchery is a meal planning app. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it. The short version: your meal plans, grocery lists, prices, recipes and settings stay on your device. We do not run ads, we do not track you, and you don't need an account.

Who we are

Kitchery is published by Armandas Juscius, an individual developer based in Lithuania (EU), who is the data controller for the limited information described below. Contact: kitchery.support@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your device

Your household settings, food preferences, allergies you choose to enter, meal plans, grocery lists, prices you type in, spending totals, your own recipes and what the app learns about your taste are stored only on your device (with a backup in your device's app storage). We cannot see this information and we do not receive it. Deleting the app, or using You › Delete all my data, removes it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see your name, email or payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), a subscription service that acts as our processor. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created on your device, your purchase and subscription information (such as product, purchase date, trial and expiry), basic technical information (such as app version, operating system version and App Store country) and the IP address used for the connection. This information is not linked to your name or email and is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Why we use it (legal basis)

We use subscription information only to provide the subscription you bought, restore it on a new device and handle support requests. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) the legal basis is the performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and answering your questions (Article 6(1)(f)).

International transfers

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Subscription records are kept while your subscription is active and afterwards only as long as needed for accounting, legal obligations and resolving disputes. Support emails are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Email kitchery.support@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or health data.

Children

Kitchery is intended for adults planning meals for their households. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health information

Allergy and diet settings are used only on your device to filter recipes. Always check ingredient labels. Kitchery is not medical or nutritional advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email kitchery.support@gmail.com.
